# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place to learn and contribute, regardless of
experience level, background, or how you found it.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful in issues, PRs, and reviews. Disagree with the code, not the person.
- Give feedback that a first-time contributor can act on: what to change and why.
- Assume good faith. Most mistakes are honest ones.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in any project space.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions, or reviews.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, open an issue, or if the report itself
needs to stay private, contact the maintainer directly through their GitHub profile.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or PRs, or block contributors who violate this
Code of Conduct. Enforcement decisions are made at maintainer discretion and can be appealed
by reaching out directly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
